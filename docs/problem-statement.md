# Problem statement

Security analysts often receive fragmented authentication, endpoint, system,
and network telemetry. An alert may identify a suspicious event without
showing what happened before it, which assets are connected, whether the
evidence supports competing explanations, or whether a response actually
worked.

TraceSOC helps an analyst investigate a suspected account compromise by
normalizing related events, reconstructing a timeline, linking evidence to
entities and findings, and proposing a safe, simulated response. The analyst
must be able to inspect the source evidence behind material claims and see a
clear distinction between observed facts, inferences, and unresolved
hypotheses.

## Primary user

The primary user is a security analyst reviewing a candidate account-
compromise incident in a local, synthetic demonstration environment.

## Product promise

> Don't just detect the alert. Reconstruct the attack, show the evidence, and
> verify the response.
